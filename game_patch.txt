2. Add Game.improve_skill():

def improve_skill(self, skill, amount=1):
    self.s['skills'][skill] += amount
    if self.s['skills'][skill] % 10 == 0:
        self.say(f"{skill} increased to {self.s['skills'][skill]}!")

3. In _xp() level-up block add:
